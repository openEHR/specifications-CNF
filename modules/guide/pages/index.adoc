= {spec_title} [.spec-status.spec-status-development]#{spec_status}#

Comprehensive guide to the openEHR conformance framework. Covers conformance profiles, test categories, assessment methodology, and the certification process for platform implementations. It explains how conformance profiles are structured, what a test schedule contains, and what evidence is required to obtain a conformance certificate. Platform vendors and procurement teams use this guide to understand what compliant behaviour means and how it is verified.

== Purpose

This guide describes the openEHR Conformance Teting artefacts and methodology. The audience of this document includes:

* Software development organisations developing HIT systems;
* Customer organisations.
